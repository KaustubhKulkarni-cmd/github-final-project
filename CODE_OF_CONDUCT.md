# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting different opinions and experiences.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.
